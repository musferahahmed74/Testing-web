## How to Contribute

Ready to build something?

1. **Fork** this repository.
2. Create a new branch for your challenge, for example `challenge/your-challenge-name`.
3. Add your challenge using the required format and template.
4. Open a **Pull Request** describing your challenge.
5. A maintainer will review it and may ask you to make changes.
6. Once approved, your challenge will be merged into the repository and added to TMON.

For the full submission requirements, see the **[Contribution Guidelines](https://github.com/musferahahmed74/Testing-web/blob/main/Contribution%20guidelines.md)**.

Every approved and merged challenge counts toward your contributor recognition and rewards.

##  Pre-Submission Guidelines

Before submitting a challenge, please make sure it:

* Is your own work, or properly credits any source it was inspired by
* Has a clear title and description
* Has a clear objective or learning outcome
* Includes the required files and information
* Follows the challenge template provided in this repository
* Includes a solution or other information needed for maintainers to verify it
* Does not contain offensive, harmful, or plagiarized content
* Has been tested before submission
* Is submitted through a **Pull Request**

Submissions that don't meet the requirements may be sent back for changes before they can be approved.
